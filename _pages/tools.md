Small research tools I built for my own work and made public in case they are useful to others.

### [X-ray Archive Finder](/tools/xray-archive-finder/)

Type a source name (any SIMBAD identifier) or coordinates and see how many observations exist in the NASA HEASARC archive for each X-ray mission — Chandra, XMM-Newton, Swift, NuSTAR, NICER, XRISM, IXPE, INTEGRAL, Suzaku, RXTE, ROSAT, ASCA, BeppoSAX, EXOSAT, Ginga and more — with dates, exposure times and observation IDs. It also shows the field in optical, X-ray and radio sky surveys, ROSAT/eROSITA all-sky survey coverage, and the SDSS optical spectrum when one exists.
