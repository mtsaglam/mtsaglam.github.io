--- layout: archive title: "X-ray Archive Finder" permalink: /tools/xray-archive-finder/ author_profile: true ---

Enter a source name or coordinates to see which X-ray missions have observed it and how many times, from the NASA HEASARC archive. Names are resolved with SIMBAD, so any of the object's identifiers work. Click a mission to list its observations, or download everything as CSV. Open full screen.

The observation index is rebuilt every night from the HEASARC master tables, so observations added today appear tomorrow. Source code: github.com/mtsaglam/xray-archive-finder.
